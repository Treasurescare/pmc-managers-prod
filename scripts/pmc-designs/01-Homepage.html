How it works

From draft to signed contract,
on one sealed record.

Seven stages, one platform. Pick a stage to see what happens there and what proof it leaves behind.

Stage I · Draft

AI tender builder

Upload a DPR and the builder drafts the notice, covers, fees and EMD in minutes. Start from master templates or clone a past tender. Every draft is versioned.

Written to the ledger asVersion history · v2
Stage II · Publish

Global notice board

One click publishes to the public board with a reference number, delivery country and deadline. Suppliers whose KYC matches are alerted at once.

Written to the ledger asnotice.publish · #1872
Stage III · Clarify

Clarifications & corrigenda

Suppliers ask questions in the portal; answers and corrigenda go to every bidder with an effective date, so no one bids on stale terms.

Written to the ledger ascorrigendum.issue · #1880
Stage IV · Bid

Sealed two-envelope bids

Technical and commercial envelopes are sealed until the deadline. EMD is paid to escrow or lodged as a bank guarantee inside the bid.

Written to the ledger asbid.seal · #1891
Stage V · Evaluate

Committees with quorum

Form a committee, invite independent external evaluators, and score technically before any price is opened. The AI anomaly check flags outlier bids.

Written to the ledger ascommittee.form · #1895
Stage VI · Compare

L1 commercial comparison

Commercial envelopes open side by side with the lowest evaluated bid ranked first, in each bid's own currency.

Written to the ledger asenvelope.open · #1899
Stage VII · Award

Award & e-signature

Award letters, POs and contracts go out for DocuSign signature, in the portal or by email. Contracts and milestones stay tracked after award.

How do I register as a supplier?

Choose Register as supplier, enter your company details, then upload the KYC documents the form lists for your country and category. You can save and resume at any point. PMC reviews the file and you are notified when your KYC is approved.

Which documents does KYC need?

The document list depends on your country and the licences your work category requires (for example, electrical or plumbing licences for Kazakhstan works packages). The registration form shows exactly which slots apply to you.

How are sealed bids kept confidential?

Bids are submitted in separate technical and commercial envelopes. Envelopes open only after the submission deadline, and only for the evaluation committee. Every open, view and decision is written to a SHA-256 hash-chained audit ledger.

Can I change or withdraw my bid?

If the tender allows withdrawal, you can withdraw or resubmit before the submission deadline from My bids. The tender page states whether withdrawal is allowed.

How will I hear about a corrigendum?

When a tender you follow or have bid on is amended, you get an alert in the portal and by email, and the change is shown on the tender page with its effective date.

What is EMD and how do I pay it?

The Earnest Money Deposit secures your bid. Each tender sets it as a fixed amount or a percentage. Pay it from the bid tracker by transfer to escrow or by uploading a bank guarantee. Escrow confirms receipt before your bid moves on.
