DESCRIPTION
PMC Managers – Global Tender Grid brings buyers and suppliers together on one audited procurement platform.

FOR SUPPLIERS
• Browse live tenders from PMC buyers and official public sources worldwide, with the original source and reference on each one
• Save alerts by keyword, country, value and buyer, and get a push notification as soon as a matching tender is published
• Register once, keep your company documents in one place and submit sealed bids securely
• AI tender tools: summarise a tender document and check your bid readiness before you submit
• Track clarifications, deadlines and award results

FOR BUYERS
• Publish tenders and receive sealed bids that nobody can open before the deadline
• Every step is recorded in an audit trail

ON YOUR IPHONE
• New-tender alerts
• Lock the app with Face ID
• Share any tender with your team
• Works in 14 languages, including Arabic, Chinese, Hindi, Japanese and Korean

An account is free to create. Some services, such as publishing tenders or AI tools, may carry fees shown before you pay.

NOTES FOR APP REVIEW (paste into App Review Information > Notes)
PMC Managers is the official app of the PMC Managers Global Tender Grid (www.pmcmanagers.com), operated by THE PMC MANAGERS LIMITED.
Native features in this app:
1. Push notifications: after signing in as a supplier, open the gear button (bottom-left) > "Turn on new-tender alerts". Suppliers receive a push when a newly published tender matches one of their saved alerts (Supplier portal > Saved alerts).
2. Face ID lock: gear button > "Lock the app with Face ID".
3. Native share sheet: the share button (bottom-left) shares the current tender page.
4. Offline screen when there is no connection.
Account deletion: Supplier portal > Account > "Delete my account" (also linked from the gear button).
