HOW IT WORKS

A seat for both sides of the table.

  1. I Build the tender from a templateScope, BOQ, eligibility and evaluation weights, routed through your own approval chain.
  2. II Publish and manage clarificationsOne notice reaches registered vendors in the right categories. Every answer goes to all bidders.
  3. III Receive sealed bidsSubmissions stay encrypted until the opening time — no early looks, no disputes about late bids.
  4. IV Evaluate, sign and awardCommittee members score side by side, sign off, and the award is issued with a full audit pack.

THE LIFECYCLE

Seven stages. Nothing off the record.

Each stage has an owner, a deadline and a permission set. Moving forward needs the right role, and every move is written to the audit log.

  1. I DraftScope, BOQ and eligibility approved.
  2. II PublishLive with a hard deadline.
  3. III ClarifyOne answer, every bidder.
  4. IV Seal● NOWEncrypted until opening.
  5. V EvaluateWeighted, signed scoring.
  6. VI OpenQualified bids ranked.
  7. VII AwardLetter of award, result public.
