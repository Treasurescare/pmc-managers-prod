Frequently asked
How are sealed bids kept confidential?

Bids are submitted in separate technical and commercial envelopes. Envelopes open only after the submission deadline, and only for the evaluation committee. Every open, view and decision is written to a SHA-256 hash-chained audit ledger.

Can I change or withdraw my bid?

If the tender allows withdrawal, you can withdraw or resubmit before the submission deadline from My bids. The tender page states whether withdrawal is allowed.

How will I hear about a corrigendum?

When a tender you follow or have bid on is amended, you get an alert in the portal and by email, and the change is shown on the tender page with its effective date.

What is EMD and how do I pay it?

The Earnest Money Deposit secures your bid. Each tender sets it as a fixed amount or a percentage. Pay it from the bid tracker by transfer to escrow or by uploading a bank guarantee. Escrow confirms receipt before your bid moves on.

Which currencies are supported?

Budgets and bids are shown in the tender's own currency, for example USD, KZT or INR. The tender states whether multi-currency bids are allowed.
